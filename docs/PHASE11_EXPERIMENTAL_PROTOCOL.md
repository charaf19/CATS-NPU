# Phase 11 experimental status and stopping record

**2026-10-29 — Closed at the novelty gate. No confirmatory protocol was frozen or activated.** This document records what was executed, what was not authorized by the scientific gates, and which comparisons would be necessary to support claims. It is not a preregistration, an unexecuted promised campaign, or permission to consume a final holdout.

**Completed work.** Primary-source review and code inspection; source/preprocessing inventory; SHA-256/MD5 verification of both BEIR archives; package and device discovery; and CPU analytical checks. Source and outputs are in `results/phase11/20261029/`. No relevance judgments or score-array contents were read by the inventory. No embedding model inference or NPU scoring inference was executed. The analytical score witness and rounding checks are not real retrieval experiments.

**Partitions and leakage.** The historical development IDs are SciFact `{1,3,5,13}` and FiQA `{8,15,18,26}`. They are previously used development data, not a new holdout. The historical Phase 5 `confirmatory_holdout` labels were consumed in later studies; those labels do not confer untouched status for Phase 11. No new calibration/development/final partition was selected, no final relevance judgments were used, and no new query was encoded or ranked. Corpus access for a future calibration would be a transductive assumption to disclose, not proof of query generalization. SIFT1M's Euclidean task remains distinct and was not repurposed.

**Mandatory comparator status.** No Phase 11 scoring method was run. The applicable controls remain: original embeddings under the actual low-precision scorer; an independently justified wider-precision reference; seeded random and structured orthogonal transforms; applicable published calibration/quantization; reconstruction-trained transforms; a simple rank-aware transform; and any distinct proposed method. The last category was not identified, so an eight-arm benchmark would produce no mechanism-isolation evidence. LLM INT4, PQ codebooks and FP16 GEMM cannot be treated as interchangeable “same precision” implementations. A published method with changed encoder weights or geometry would require its own scope qualification rather than being silently reproduced by a rotation.

Higher-precision scoring, including the ACL 2026 HPS control, must specify original versus rounded operands, actual accumulation/output behavior and total online costs. Rescoring only returned tied items is not automatically exact with respect to original Top-K: a true winner may already have been excluded by earlier error. No benefit or comparable-cost resolution by these controls is asserted without measurement.

**Metric contract retained without manufacturing observations.** Top-K overlap is `|T_K(reference) intersect T_K(method)|/K`; exact membership agreement is a separate Boolean. Ordered agreement includes the deterministic index tie rule. Near-boundary pairwise inversion comparisons require a prespecified pair set/window, and score ties should be reported separately from strict reversals. A margin-stratified analysis must not choose favorable bins after outcomes. nDCG@10 and Recall@K against dataset judgments measure relevance, not numerical fidelity; neither was calculated here.

End-to-end latency would start with the defined host query embedding and end with host ranked IDs. It must include online transformation, staging/transfers, scoring, output, selection and any validation or reranking. Corpus preprocessing and calibration time are offline costs to report separately; resident preprocessed corpus assumptions must match across arms. Repeated timings in one process do not create independent system sessions. No energy-consumption or peak hardware-memory metric is introduced.

**Confirmation status.** Primary hypothesis: **none frozen**. Primary metric/effect threshold: **none selected after evidence**. Final holdout: **none accessed or designated**. Independent sessions, paired query-level analysis, bootstrap intervals, multiple-comparison handling and mechanism ablations: **not run**. There are no P values or confidence intervals to report. Zero mismatches in a deterministic scalar audit is not a statistical generalization result.

**Stopping decision.** The proposed transform/loss is the simple rank-aware baseline, and the alternative dyadic family gives no new normal-range product capability. Gate A fails before numerical feasibility and calibration development. No single hardware run can establish the absent mechanism distinction. The present study therefore ends with the [NO-GO decision](PHASE11_FINAL_RESEARCH_DECISION.md), preserving all original records. Any reopening requires a concrete new technical property and a new gate assessment, rather than executing this document as a benchmark plan.
